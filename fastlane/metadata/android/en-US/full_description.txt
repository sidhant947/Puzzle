Built with Flutter, this premium suite features over 304 unique games across 6 critical cognitive areas, all completely offline, ad-free, and open-source.

Whether you want to play a quick game of Minesweeper, challenge yourself with 2048, solve a classic Sudoku, or boost your memory, PUZZLE has you covered.
