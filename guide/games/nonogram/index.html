Nonogram

Solving guide and strategies for Nonogram.

Objective & Rules

Shade or partition the grid according to numerical clues, ensuring all shaded/unshaded regions form valid continuous networks.

Controls & Interaction

  • Tap cells on the board to toggle states, mark cells, or input values.

Step-by-Step Gameplay Walkthrough

  1. Inspect Clues: Locate numbers or grid markings that define region sizes or constraints.
  2. Shade/Draw: Tap cells to shade them or drag borders to partition grids.
  3. Connect: Ensure continuous paths are maintained according to rules (e.g. shaded cells must connect in Nurikabe).
  4. Solve: Satisfy all region boundaries and cell states to complete the level.

Solving Strategies & Tips

  • Separate adjacent clue numbers immediately; they cannot belong to the same region.
  • Work from the outer edges inward, as borders restrict region growth options.
  • Check for isolated cells or dead-ends where paths could get trapped.
