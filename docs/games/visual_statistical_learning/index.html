Visual Statistical Learning

Solving guide and strategies for Visual Statistical Learning.

Objective & Rules

Memorize and recall coordinates, timeline events, or object displacements on the screen.

Controls & Interaction

  • Tap buttons or interactive items on the screen to play.

Step-by-Step Gameplay Walkthrough

  1. Examine Layout: Study the positions, attributes, or timeline order of items shown.
  2. Scramble: Wait for the items to shuffle, vanish, or move.
  3. Reconstruct: Tap or drag items back to their original positions or order.
  4. Verify: Confirm your layout to score points.

Solving Strategies & Tips

  • Focus on the relationships between items rather than individual details.
  • Anchor items to screen landmarks (borders, corners).
  • Group similar elements together to simplify recall.
