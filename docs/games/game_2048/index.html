Game 2048

Solving guide and strategies for Game 2048.

Objective & Rules

Identify arithmetic relations, factor numbers, or solve equations to match target mathematical goals.

Controls & Interaction

  • Swipe or drag across the grid to shift elements or move tokens.

Step-by-Step Gameplay Walkthrough

  1. Read Target: Check the target value or formula displayed at the top.
  2. Solve: Compute the combinations of grid numbers or operations that resolve to the target.
  3. Select: Tap the matching numbers or drag operator symbols into place.
  4. Succeed: Resolve the required number of equations before the timer expires.

Solving Strategies & Tips

  • Look for multiples or divisor pairs when dealing with multiplication/division targets.
  • Examine odd/even parities to instantly discard invalid options.
  • Work backwards from the target number to simplify math options.
